The reason for this project is just to give you context about me as a person. I am a person who wants to build a business, and it's really hard for me to find people with a similar mindset and goals to mine. That's why I built this application: not to create a group, but for you to describe yourself as the type of person you are. You would be matched with certain people who match your personality or mindset, and you would be put in a group where you can converse about the things that you're interested in and what type of person you are.

The idea is that there's an AI which will group you together depending on how you react and what your messages are in the chat. That is the idea. Regardless of what niche you are in or what industries you are in, there is certainly a part that you might have a personal interest in that's not really related to your industry. If you are looking for loved ones and you are looking for specific qualities, you can filter it out.

Later on, I'll also be adding more features, like for you to specifically look for people that are that way and to describe yourself better so that this pro application can continue to ask you to describe yourself better. That would also enhance the overall experience of all people.
